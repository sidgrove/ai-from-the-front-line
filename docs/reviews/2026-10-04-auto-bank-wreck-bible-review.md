# Auto Bank Wreck: hostile Bible review

Reviewed 04/10/2026 against `docs/BIBLE.md` (Part 1 DNA, the 13 questions, the critique lenses, Part 2 Never list) plus Dave's own slide rules (no monospace, cut copy hard). All 58 slides seen live in the built-in browser at 16:9; fonts, dashes, capitals, emoji and logo corners checked by script across every slide. Slide 42 is mid-redesign and gets a brief note only.

## 1. Verdict: 5/10

The deck has a real spine and some genuinely good moments (the cold open on 2, the two-runs demo on 12, the asbestos strikethrough on 21, "Approve future mistakes" on 23, the Xero as-deployed / optimised pair on 54 to 56), but they are buried in 58 slides when about 40 would land harder. Too much of it is the same few templates repeated: nine emoji icon-tile lists, eight near-identical "screenshot plus Slack card" app slides nobody in the room can read, and ten text-only beat or divider slides with faint decorative rings. Several of the strongest facts live only in the speaker notes, and one screenshot (45) is full of the monospace tracked capitals Dave hates, so on "nothing to add, nothing to subtract" and "is this where fun goes to die" it fails as a whole even where single slides pass.

## 4. Deck-wide patterns

**Fonts.** No computed monospace anywhere in live text: good. But screenshots smuggle it in (45 heavily, 51 cell refs), which on a projector reads exactly as the typewriter type Dave hates. The Xero mocks on 55 and 56 and the dashboard tabs on 42 fall back to Helvetica/Arial; defensible as a Xero imitation on 55/56, not on 42. The serif is used well for titles; serif italic numerals on 57 are decoration.

**Components.** Three templates do most of the work: the white emoji-tile list card (9 slides), screenshot plus Slack card (6 slides in a row), and "What I use / Other options" (29, 48, 49, 51). Each is fine once; repeated, they turn the talk into a catalogue. Floating status chips ("Use daily", "Use to build", "Non-daily") hang off the title row on 29, 46, 51, 52, 53 and add nothing the section label does not already say. Title height drifts (48 and 49 sit lower than everything else).

**Colour.** Mostly means something (green tick vs red cross on 27, as-deployed vs optimised on 55/56). Lapses: red saturated white-text buttons three times as a joke device, coloured left stripes on 55/56, glow on 21, radial gradient on 24, orbs on 1 and 58, decorative rings on every beat slide.

**Text density.** Most slides are lean. The heavy ones are the ones that matter most: 55 (83 words), 56 (99), 53 (69), 57 (69), 52 (66), 27 (66). Kickers and subtitles under titles recur (9, 21, 29, 33, 52, 56) and are the first thing to cut.

**Joy moments.** Present and earned: the W/k scribble on the cover, "I Accept My Redundancy", "(Source: vibes based)", the asbestos strikethrough, "Approve future mistakes", the Xero magnifier. Too much: the 3D eyes on 4 and the shush emoji on 18 (stickers, not wit). Missing: a payoff. The cover promises "Rec" becoming "Wreck" and the deck never closes the loop; 56 or 58 should flip it back ("Auto Bank Rec. Done properly.") as the one considered moment at the finish.

**Narrative.** Three acts are clear, but the corner labels mislead: 21 to 25 say "What works · the pitch" while arguing it does not work. "Just use it" (15) lands in the middle of the case against. Two "so what now" beats (20, 26) and two "what is it / why a problem" beats (7, 11) slow the turns. The app section is ten slides long against a two-slide spotlight that is the talk's namesake; rebalance so the finale gets the time.

**Language.** British spelling holds. Lapses listed in fix 8.
